Frequently Asked Questions

Are your calculators really free?
Yes — every calculator on CalcWorks is 100% free, with no sign-up, no downloads and no usage limits. The site is supported by non-intrusive advertising and optional affiliate recommendations.
How accurate are the calculators?
Tools use standard, industry-proven formulas (amortization, Mifflin-St Jeor, WHO BMI categories, official unit conversion factors and more). Results are rounded sensibly and cross-checked against reference data.
Can I use the calculators on my phone?
Absolutely. The entire site is mobile-first, so calculations, charts and tables work beautifully on phones, tablets and desktops alike.
Is the site available in other languages?
The interface is built to international standards and currently supports English. A full multilingual rollout — driven by translations rather than separate sites — is part of the roadmap.
Do you have a calculator that's not listed?
Tell us! Head to the contact page and request a tool. Popular requests are added quickly thanks to the platform's reusable calculator architecture.
Can I embed or link to a calculator?
Linking is always welcome. For advance permission to embed or redistribute, please contact us — most requests are granted.
